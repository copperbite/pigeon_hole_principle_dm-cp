## Academic & Mathematical Inspirations

- **d3graphTheory** (MIT License): Interactive graph-theory visual guidance and mathematical pedagogy.
- **Dirichlet's approximation theorem**: P. G. Lejeune Dirichlet (1834), *Schubfachprinzip* (the drawer principle).
- **Erdős-Szekeres Theorem**: Paul Erdős and George Szekeres (1935), "A combinatorial problem in geometry", *Compositio Mathematica*.
- **Ramsey Theory**: Frank P. Ramsey (1930), "On a problem of formal logic", *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society*.
- **Havel-Hakimi Algorithm**: Václav Havel (1955) and S. Louis Hakimi (1962).
- **Hall's Marriage Theorem**: Philip Hall (1935), "On Representatives of Subsets", *J. London Math. Soc.*.
